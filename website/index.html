A few good things to know.

Will it change my Codex setup?+

The manager keeps its account vault separate and does not rewrite your Codex configuration. Switching changes the active sign-in and gracefully restarts Codex if it is open. Local projects and chat history stay in place; cloud conversations and permissions depend on the selected account.

Can I go back to one account?+

Yes. Choose “Return to official Codex” in the app. Keep your current sign-in or restore the original saved sign-in, then keep or remove the manager’s encrypted vault. Its startup entry and automatic switching are disabled as part of that flow.

Can I switch while Codex is working?+

Finish the current task first. Automatic switching waits for Codex to be idle and pauses if activity cannot be verified. Switching accounts does not continue a model response already in progress.
